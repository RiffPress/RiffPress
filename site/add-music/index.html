Before you start

You need a WordPress site. WordPress is free software that runs on a web host. Most hosts set it up in a few clicks, and any host will do as long as it runs WordPress 7.2 or newer. Once it is up, you sign in at your site's address followed by /wp-admin. That is the dashboard, where everything below happens.

Then install Riff. In the dashboard, go to Plugins, then Add New Plugin, then Upload Plugin, choose the zip, and activate it.

Get RiffTry it first in the demo

The demo is a WordPress site running in your browser with Riff already installed. Its dashboard is at /wp-admin too, so you can follow every step here without a site of your own.

Make your Listen page

The Listen page is where your music lives: every album as a cover, browsing by album, artist and track, and one search box. The music keeps playing while visitors move around it.

After you activate Riff, the Dashboard shows a notice. Press Create Listen page and it's done. To make it yourself:

  1. Go to Pages, then Add Page, and title it Listen, or whatever you like.
  2. Click the empty line, type /riff and choose the Riff block.
  3. Press Publish.

To make it the front page of the site, go to Settings, then Reading, choose A static page, and pick it as the homepage.

Add app/ to the page's address, for example /listen/app/, and the same page opens as a player on its own, in Riff's dark look and without your theme. A browser can install that address as an app.

Upload your music

  1. Go to Media, then Add New Media File.
  2. Drag in an album's tracks, all at once. MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV and OGG all work.

That's it. Open your Listen page and the album is there, with its cover, ready to play. Tap a track and the player bar appears along the bottom. It keeps playing while you scroll, open another page, or lock your phone. Tap the bar for Now Playing.

Titles, artists and covers come from the files

Riff reads the title, artist, album and cover art from the tags inside each file, the same tags your music app uses. It also shows each track's quality, such as MP3 · 320 kbps or FLAC · 24-bit 96 kHz.

If a file has no tags, or the wrong ones, go to Media, then Library, click the file and fix them there. The same screen has two fields Riff adds. Artwork is a cover for that track. Video version is a video in the library, for a song that has one; listeners can switch between the song and the video while it plays. A video file has a Captions field of its own, for a WebVTT file in the library, which listeners can turn on in Now Playing.

If an album has no cover at all, Riff draws one: a record with one groove per track, each groove as long as the track. The demo's albums wear them.

Give an album its own post

To write about an album, or to play it on its own page with the rest of your site around it, put it in a post:

  1. Go to Posts, then Add Post, and type the album's name as the title.
  2. Click the empty line below it, type /playlist and press Enter.
  3. Choose the tracks from the Media Library, or upload them here. Drag them into order.
  4. Press Set featured image in the sidebar to give it a cover, then Publish.

An Audio block for each track works too: type /audio instead.

Make the post private, a draft, or password protected, and its tracks stay off the Listen page for visitors until you publish it.

Many albums at once

If you have a folder per album and your host gives you WP-CLI, wp riff import with the path to the folder uploads everything and gives each album a post. A cover image in the folder becomes the artwork. Run it again and it skips what it already imported; add --dry-run to see what it would do first.
