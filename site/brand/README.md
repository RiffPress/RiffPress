# Riff's brand

**The wordmark** is the word *Riff*: *Ri* in EB Garamond Italic at weight 800, and its *ff* as a score writes *fortissimo*, music's marking for very loud. The *ff* is Bravura's (the SMuFL reference font for music notation), scaled so its *f*s rise to Garamond's ascender, and set close so its tails sweep under the *i*. In a score, dynamics sit beside a text face, so the pair is how music is printed. Every file is outlines, so nothing depends on either font being installed.

**The main mark** is the word in charcoal ink on Riff's warm paper, `#fcfaf6`: the RiffPress organisation's picture (`avatar-ink.png`), the social preview, and the header of every page on source.riffpress.com, where the word leads back to riffpress.com and *Source* beside it to the docs. **The app icon** is the same word on its charcoal tile, for the player: the installed app, the plugin's icon and the favicon, which uses the tile cropped to its edge so it fills a small square. Those are the two; there is no other mark.

**Proportions** follow the golden ratio, φ. On the app icon, the word's margins either side are 1/φ⁵ of the tile.

**Colour** is Riff's amber, `#ffb020`, on charcoal: `#1e1e1e`, or the app's `#121212`. On the paper, amber is too pale to read as text, so links and outline buttons there use a deep amber, `#8f5400` (5.9:1 on the paper), and the focus ring is the same. Filled buttons stay amber, with charcoal on them, and a page has one. There is no blue: on the site the colour comes from the player, in its pictures and its covers.

**Type** is wordpress.org's: EB Garamond for display and headings, Inter for text.
