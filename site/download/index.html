Requirements

  • WordPress7.0 or newer
  • PHP8.1 or newer
  • ThemeAny theme. The Listen page template needs a block theme.
  • Anything elseNothing else. There is no build step and no settings screen.

Install it

  1. In your site's dashboard, go to Plugins, then Add New Plugin, then Upload Plugin.
  2. Choose the zip you downloaded and press Install Now.
  3. Press Activate Plugin. Every post with audio or video in it now plays through Riff.

New to WordPress? Add your music starts from the beginning and ends with an album on your site.

Update it

Riff is not on the WordPress.org plugin directory, so the Plugins screen will not offer updates on its own. To update, download the new zip and upload it the same way. WordPress notices the plugin is already installed and offers to replace the current version with the uploaded one. Your posts, files and settings are untouched.

Riff stores little of its own: the link between a song and its video version, the album and artist terms it files from your tags, and listeners' playlists, likes and what they played. Deleting the plugin from the Plugins screen removes those and leaves your files, their tags and their images alone.
