Riff turns the music in your WordPress Media Library into a streaming player on your own site, with albums, search, a queue and the lock screen.

Try the demo or download Riff {{version}}

From the Media Library to the lock screen

Upload an album's files to the Media Library. Riff reads the title, artist, album and cover from the tags inside them, the same tags your music app uses, so there is nothing to fill in.

Put the Riff block on a page and that page becomes your library: every album as a cover, the artists and tracks, and one search box. Press a track and the player bar appears. It keeps playing while people read the rest of your site, and it sits on their lock screen with the cover and the controls.

  • A queue you can reorder, likes, and playlists for people who sign in.
  • A song's video version, a tap away, at the same point in the song.
  • AirPlay and Chromecast, where the browser has them.
  • An app/ address that installs on a phone or a computer.
  • No account, no tracking and no outside service. The files stay on your host.
