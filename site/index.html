Publish like Bandcamp. Listen like Spotify.

One site, two sides. You run it from the WordPress Media Library, and your listeners get a player that works like the streaming apps they already use.

You publish from WordPress

Upload your tracks to the Media Library and put them in a post. WordPress reads the titles, artists and artwork from the files, and they stay on your server at the quality you uploaded. Make a post private and its tracks stay out of the library. There's no other account to sign up for and nobody takes a cut.

Listeners get a streaming player

They can browse albums and artists, search, line up a queue, like songs, albums and artists into Your Library, which follows their account when they sign in, and make playlists of their own. Now Playing works from the lock screen, with AirPlay and Cast, and the music keeps going while they read the rest of your site. The Listen page can also be installed as an app.

Riff publishes and plays your music. It doesn't sell music or take payments. Bandcamp and Spotify are trademarks of their owners, and Riff isn't connected to either.

Keeps playing while you read

Put a record on and keep reading. Scroll, open another post, lock your phone; the music carries on.

  • Swipe the barLeft or right for the next or previous track. Down to stop.
  • Lock screen controlsTitle, artwork and the play controls on the phone's lock screen, and on the media keys.
  • Keeps playing between pagesFollow a link to another page on the site and the music keeps going. Reload, and it is paused where it was.
  • The filamentThe amber line in the bar is a level meter. It brightens with the music and fades when you pause.
  • AirPlay and ChromecastA cast button appears in Now Playing, and in the bar on a phone, when the browser finds a speaker or TV on the network.
  • Keyboard shortcutsSpace or K plays and pauses, J and L skip ten seconds, Shift+N and Shift+P change track.

The whole post, up next

Every track in the post lines up behind the one you chose, the way an album plays through. Pull the handle to see what's coming.

Add the Riff Lyrics plugin and a Lyrics tab sits beside Up next, with the words from each file's lyrics tag or typed in the Media Library.

Song or video, same track

When a song has a video, such as a live take, a switch at the top of Now Playing flips between the record and the room without losing your place.

One page where everything plays

Add the Riff block to a page and it becomes your shelf. Open any album and play from its cover; what is playing stays playing while you browse.

  • Albums, artists and tracksRiff files every audio file in the Media Library by the album and artist in its tags, so listeners can browse it three ways.
  • SearchOne search box finds tracks by title, artist or album.
  • Only what you publishedTracks from a draft, private or password-protected post stay out of browse and search for visitors.
  • Quality on every trackEach track shows what WordPress read from the file, such as MP3 · 320 kbps or FLAC · 24-bit 96 kHz.
  • A player of its ownAdd app/ to the Listen page's address for the player without your theme, which a browser can install as an app.
  • Share a trackShare sends a link to the track that is playing, and the link opens on that track without starting it.

Get Riff

Riff plays the audio, video and playlist blocks WordPress already has, from your own Media Library. One new page, if you want it.

Download RiffAdd your music

Version {{version}}, as a zip for the Plugins screen. Upload it under Plugins, Add New Plugin, and activate.

What it needs

  • WordPress 7.0 or newer
  • PHP 8.1 or newer
  • Any theme; the Listen page template needs a block theme
  • Nothing to configure
  • No outside services: nothing is sent anywhere but your own site

What it adds

  • One script, one stylesheet, no build step
  • Artwork and Video version fields on media
  • Albums and artists, filed from each file's tags
  • The Riff block, or [riff]
  • wp riff import, to add a folder of albums at once

Open

  • Source on GitHub
  • How it works, in the readme
  • GPLv2 or later, like WordPress
  • Release notes
  • Report a problem

For developers

  • riff_track on every media item in REST
  • riff/v1/library, plus tracks, albums and artists with search, and users/me/likes, users/me/history and users/me/playlists for the signed-in listener
  • WPGraphQL fields, when it is installed
  • A developer reference for every hook, the JavaScript API, REST and CSS, generated from the code
  • Your own tabs beside Up next, the way Riff Lyrics adds one
  • Your own source of tracks, through the Riff\Source interface
