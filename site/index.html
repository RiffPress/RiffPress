Own every note

Riff turns the music in your WordPress Media Library into a streaming player on your own site, with albums, search, a queue and the lock screen.

Try the demo or download Riff {{version}}

From the Media Library to the lock screen

Upload an album. Riff reads its tags, makes a page of it, and keeps it playing from page to page, all the way to the lock screen.

  • Queue, likes and playlists
  • Video versions
  • AirPlay and Chromecast
  • Installs as an app
  • Your files stay on your host

Every album has a cover

When an album has none, Riff draws one from the record itself: a groove for each track, as long as the track, wavering with its music.

The palette and the layout come from the album's name, so no two are alike.

Now Playing, on a wide screen

The cover, the rest of the album in Up next, and the bar that never leaves.
