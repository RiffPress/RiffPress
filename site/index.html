Press play on your own site.

The songs and videos in a post, in a player that feels like the apps you already use.

Try the live demoPut it on your site

The bar

Keeps playing while you read.

Tap a track and it starts. Scroll on, open another post, lock the phone; the music carries on.

  • Swipe to skipSideways on the bar for the next or previous track.
  • Lock screenArtwork, title and controls where your phone puts them.
  • Picks up againReload and it's paused where you left it.
  • A wire that glowsThe bar's amber line lights with the music, and cools when you pause.
  • AirPlay and CastSend it to the speakers when they're in reach.
  • KeyboardSpace plays and pauses, J and L skip ten seconds.

Up next

The whole post, queued.

Every track in the post lines up behind the one you chose. Pull the handle to see what's coming.

Song or video

Same track, two ways.

When a song has a video, a switch at the top of Now Playing flips between them without losing your place.

The Riff block

One page. Everything plays.

Add the block to a page and it becomes your library. Open any post inside it, play from a cover, and the music carries on.

What it needs

  • WordPress 6.5 or newer
  • PHP 8.1 or newer
  • Any block theme

What it adds

  • One script, one stylesheet, no build step
  • Artwork and Video version fields on media
  • A block and a shortcode for the Listen page
  • Nothing to configure

Open

  • Source on GitHub
  • GPL, like WordPress
  • Report a problem

For developers

  • riff_track on every media item in REST
  • riff/v1/library, with schemas
  • WPGraphQL fields when it's installed
  • Filters and actions, listed in the readme
