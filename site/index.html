Publish like Bandcamp. Listen like Spotify.

You run it from the WordPress Media Library. Your listeners get a player like the streaming apps they already use.

  • Your files, your siteUpload to the Media Library. Nothing to sign up for, and nobody takes a cut.
  • A real streaming playerA queue, likes, playlists, the lock screen, AirPlay and Cast.
  • Keeps playing as they browseThe music carries on across every page of your site.

Riff doesn't sell music. Bandcamp and Spotify are trademarks of their owners, and Riff isn't connected to either.

One page where everything plays

Add the Riff block to a page and it becomes your shelf of albums, artists and tracks.

  • Browse and searchAlbums, artists and tracks, filed from your files' own tags.
  • Your LibraryLiked songs, albums and artists, and playlists of their own.
  • Up nextEvery track in the post lines up behind the one you chose.
  • Song or videoFlip to a track's video without losing your place.
  • Lock screen and CastControls on the lock screen, with AirPlay and Chromecast.
  • Installs as an appIts app/ address installs on a phone or a desktop.

Made in Rochester

Riff began in September 2026 in Rochester, New York. It is a music city: George Eastman founded the Eastman School of Music here in 1921, and every June since 2002 the Rochester International Jazz Festival has filled the streets downtown. Riff is made here, so that a musician's own WordPress site can be where their music plays.
