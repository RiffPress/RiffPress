prefers-reduced-transparency

Removes the translucent wash and the bloom when the system asks.

How Riff uses it

On a phone the top of Now Playing carries a translucent wash of the filament's colour that brightens with the music. Under reduced transparency that wash is removed, and the wire and its white heart lose their blurred bloom, leaving a solid line.

Why

The wash and the bloom are the player's only translucent layers over content, and they change opacity every frame.
