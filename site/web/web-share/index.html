Web Share

Opens the system share sheet with the track's title and the post link.

How Riff uses it

The Share chip in Now Playing calls navigator.share() with the track title and the post's URL. A rejected promise from dismissing the sheet is swallowed.

Why

On a phone the system sheet is how people send a link to Messages or another app.

Without it

Falls through to the clipboard and a Copied toast.
